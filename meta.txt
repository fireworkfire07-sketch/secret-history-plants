The Forest That Behaves Like a Brain

Beneath every forest lies a hidden network of living threads — moving nutrients, carrying signals, and connecting trees in ways scientists are still trying to understand.

This is the secret history of mycelium: the underground fungal network often called the “wood wide web.”

In this cinematic nature documentary, we explore how fungi connect forests, how trees may share resources and warnings, why mycelium can solve problems without a brain, and what this hidden world reveals about nature, intelligence, and survival.
