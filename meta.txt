The Flesh of the Gods | The Secret History of Psilocybin

Two and a half thousand years ago, ancient peoples carved mushrooms into stone and called them the flesh of the gods. This is the secret history of psilocybin — a sacred mushroom of visions, worshipped by great civilizations, hunted by conquerors, and now returning to the laboratory.

In this chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we follow the sacred mushroom from Aztec ceremonies and a hidden mountain tradition to a magazine article that changed everything — and to the cautious modern science exploring it for the deepest sufferings of the mind.
