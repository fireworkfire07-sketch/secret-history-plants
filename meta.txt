The Immortal Giant | The Secret History of the Honey Fungus

Hidden beneath a forest in Oregon lives a single organism that covers three and a half square miles, weighs tens of thousands of tons, and may have been alive for nearly 9,000 years. It is not a whale. It is not a tree. It is a fungus — and it may be the largest living thing on Earth.

In this final chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we uncover the Humongous Fungus: how scientists discovered it was all one creature, how a fungus can grow essentially forever, and why the emperors chasing immortality were looking in the wrong place all along.
