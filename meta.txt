
The Root Worth More Than Gold — And Science Finally Explains Why | Secret History of Ginger
For 5,000 years, humanity trusted one twisted root as medicine — but were they right, or was it all superstition? This is the secret history of ginger: a plant so shaped by human hands it no longer exists in the wild, a spice once worth its weight in livestock, carried across oceans by ancient sailors, hoarded by kings, and hunted by empires. From the courts of ancient China and the sacred science of Ayurveda, to the markets of Venice, the voyages of Marco Polo and Columbus — and finally into the modern laboratory, where science reveals the truth about the molecules gingerol and shogaol.

By the end, one question is answered: were the ancients right about ginger?

Chapters of history, mystery, and real science — all in one root you have in your kitchen right now.
