The Plant That Refuses to Die | The Secret History of Welwitschia
In one of the driest deserts on Earth, there lives a plant that should not exist. Welwitschia mirabilis can survive for 2,000 years — outliving empires, cathedrals, and civilizations — yet it grows only TWO leaves in its entire lifetime. A living fossil from the age of the dinosaurs, its family stretches back 112 million years. This is the full story of the strangest plant on the planet: the botanist who knelt beside it in awe, the fog it drinks from the Atlantic, and the 2021 genome study that finally cracked the secret of how anything could live like this.

By the end, one question is answered: how does a plant survive for two thousand years, in a rainless desert, on just two leaves?

History, mystery, and real, verified science — from the dinosaurs to the modern genome lab.
