The Plant They Tried to Erase From History | The Secret History of Hemp
It may be the oldest crop humanity ever grew. It made the world's first paper, rigged the ships that discovered continents, and clothed founding armies. Henry Ford built a car from it said to be stronger than steel. It can even pull radiation out of poisoned soil. And then — at the peak of its promise — it was suddenly banned and almost erased from history. Why? This is the full story of hemp: from ancient China and the invention of paper, to the sailing empires, to the mysterious 1937 law that buried a "billion-dollar crop" just as new industries — timber, oil, and synthetic nylon — had everything to lose.

By the end, three mysteries are solved: who buried the most useful plant on Earth and why, what really happened to Ford's miracle car, and whether hemp can truly clean radioactive soil and fight climate change with carbon-absorbing "hempcrete." The answers are astonishing — and real.

History, mystery, and real, verified science — hidden in a plant we were made to forget.
