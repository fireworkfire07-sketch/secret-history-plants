The Mushroom of Immortality | The Secret History of Reishi

For over two thousand years, Chinese emperors searched for a single object that could grant eternal life — and again and again, the search led to one strange, shining, woody mushroom. This is Reishi, the mushroom of immortality.

In this chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we explore how a fungus too bitter to eat became the most revered medicine in East Asia, carved into art and offered to emperors — and what modern science actually found when it finally looked inside.
