The Puppet Master | The Secret History of Cordyceps

Deep in the world's rainforests, a fungus does something that sounds impossible: it takes control of a living ant's body and drives it to its death. But the real science behind the "zombie fungus" is even stranger than the legend — because for years, we got the most important part completely wrong.

In this first chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we uncover the true story of Ophiocordyceps: how it hollows out its host from the inside, why it never touches the brain, and how the same terrifying organism became a treasure worth more than gold in the mountains of Tibet — and a frontier of modern medicine.
