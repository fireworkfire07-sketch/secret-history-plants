The Monk's Mushroom | The Secret History of Lion's Mane

Centuries ago, mountain monks in Japan ate a strange white mushroom to sharpen their minds. They couldn't have known that modern science would one day find, inside it, a compound that reaches past the brain's defenses and whispers to your neurons: grow.

In this chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we uncover how Lion's Mane went from an ancient meditation aid to one of the most intriguing frontiers in the science of the aging brain — and separate the real, remarkable science from the modern hype.
