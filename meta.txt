The Puppet Master | The Secret History of Cordyceps

Deep in the mountains of Tibet and the forests of Asia grows a fungus so strange that it has inspired myths, fortunes, and scientific fascination for centuries. Cordyceps is known as the "zombie fungus" for its ability to control insects, but its history is far more extraordinary than fiction.

From ancient Tibetan medicine and imperial Chinese courts to modern laboratories and billion-dollar industries, this remarkable organism has shaped human beliefs about health, vitality, and longevity for over a thousand years.

In this episode, we uncover the secret history of Cordyceps: the fungus that became one of the most valuable medicines on Earth.
