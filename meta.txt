The Tree a Killer Murdered 184 People For | The Secret History of Sandalwood
For 3,000 years, one scent has driven prayer, greed, and murder. Sandalwood — a strange parasitic tree that feeds on its neighbors and hides its treasure deep in its heartwood — became so sacred it perfumed temples and funeral pyres, and so valuable that the notorious bandit Veerappan killed 184 people and smuggled 65 tons of it during a 36-year reign of terror that cost the Indian government a fortune to end. This is the full story of the sacred and the criminal, and the molecule science finally found inside the perfume: santalol, now studied for the very calming and healing effects ancient priests discovered by pure instinct.

By the end, one question is answered: why has a single scent held such power over the human mind for 3,000 years?

History, mystery, and real, verified science — hidden inside a scent you already know.
