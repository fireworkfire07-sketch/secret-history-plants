The Leaf an Empire Killed to Protect | The Secret History of the Mulberry
For 3,000 years, China guarded one secret with the threat of death — and it wasn't gold or a weapon. It was a leaf. The mulberry leaf, the only food of the silkworm, was the hidden key to silk: the most valuable fabric in the ancient world. This is the story of imperial secrets, monks who smuggled silkworm eggs in hollow canes in one of history's first acts of industrial espionage, and a molecule called DNJ that modern science is now confirming — proving the ancient healers may have been right all along.

By the end, one question is answered: was the grandmother's mulberry tea real medicine, or just folklore?

Three thousand years of history, mystery, and real science — hidden in a leaf you've walked past a hundred times.
