The Bread That Bewitched | The Secret History of Ergot

What if the witch trials, the visions of demons, and the blackened limbs of medieval Europe all had a single, hidden cause — growing quietly inside the daily bread? This is the story of ergot, perhaps the most historically important fungus that ever lived.

In this chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we follow ergot from the burning agony of Saint Anthony's Fire to the Salem witch trials, and finally to a Swiss laboratory where this same fungus gave birth to LSD — and to medicines still used today.
