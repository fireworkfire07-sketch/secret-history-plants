The Mind Beneath the Forest | The Secret History of Mycelium

Take one step into a forest, and beneath your foot lie kilometers of living threads — carrying messages, moving food, and connecting the trees into a single vast network. This is the secret history of mycelium: the hidden internet of the forest, and a kind of intelligence with no brain at all.

In this chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we explore the "wood wide web": how trees share resources and warnings through fungal threads, how a brainless network can solve problems, and why one of the largest and oldest living things on Earth is woven from filaments finer than hair.
