The Emperor's Poison | The Secret History of the Death Cap

It looks harmless. It even smells sweet. And it is responsible for around 90% of all fatal mushroom poisonings on Earth. This is the Death Cap — the beautiful killer that has ended the lives of emperors and kings, hidden behind the gentle face of an ordinary mushroom.

In this chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we trace the Death Cap from the assassination of a Roman emperor to the molecular level, where a single toxin quietly shuts down your cells — and discover the surprising roadside weed that became one of our only defenses against it.
