The Mold That Saved the World | The Secret History of Penicillin

A messy lab. A forgotten dish. A patch of mold that fell from nowhere. This is the secret history of penicillin — the accidental discovery that became the most important medicine in human history, and may have saved more than 80 million lives.

In this chapter of The Secret History of Fungi, we follow the mold from Fleming's cluttered bench, through a decade of neglect, to two men who smuggled it across an ocean smeared into their coats — and to the rotten melon that helped save the soldiers of D-Day.
