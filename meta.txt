The Leaf an Empire Killed to Protect | The Secret History of the Mulberry
For 3,000 years, China guarded one secret with the threat of death — and it wasn't gold, or a weapon. It was a leaf. The mulberry leaf, the only food of the silkworm, was the hidden key to silk: the most valuable fabric in the ancient world. This is the full story — the empress and the falling cocoon, a tree that fires pollen at half the speed of sound, Roman senators watching their gold drain east, and the two monks who smuggled the secret out of China inside hollow bamboo canes in one of history's first acts of industrial espionage.

And hidden inside that same leaf, modern science has found a molecule — DNJ — now confirmed in human trials to steady blood sugar, proving the ancient healers may have been right all along.

By the end, one question is answered: was the grandmother's mulberry tea real medicine, or just folklore?

Three thousand years of history, mystery, and real, verified science — hidden in a leaf you've walked past a hundred times.
